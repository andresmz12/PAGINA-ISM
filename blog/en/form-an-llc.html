How to form an LLC as a Hispanic in the U.S.: complete guide

Forming an LLC protects your personal assets, opens doors to business credit, and gives you a professional structure. We explain step by step.

ISM Consulting TeamFebruary 2025 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • An LLC separates your personal assets from the business’s.
  • The best state depends on where your business operates.
  • After forming it you need an EIN and a business bank account.
  • It is a flexible structure for small and medium businesses.

An LLC (Limited Liability Company) is the most popular business structure in the U.S. for small and medium businesses. It combines the legal protection of a corporation with the tax flexibility of a sole proprietorship.

Why form an LLC?

  • Separates your personal assets from the business — if your company has debts, your home and personal accounts are protected
  • Projects professionalism to clients, banks, and suppliers
  • Facilitates opening business bank accounts
  • Allows access to commercial credit and contracts with large companies
