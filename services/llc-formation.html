Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to form an LLC?

Usually between 3 and 7 business days, depending on the state where your company is registered.

Does an LLC make me pay less tax?

By itself, no. How it is taxed depends on how many owners it has and on what you choose. We explain it in our LLC and S-Corp guide.

Should I form the LLC in the state where I live?

It depends on where your business operates. It generally makes sense to use the state where you work, but we review your case before deciding.

Do I need to be a citizen or resident to own an LLC?

In general, citizenship or residency is not required to own an LLC, but each case depends on your situation. For immigration matters, always consult an attorney.
