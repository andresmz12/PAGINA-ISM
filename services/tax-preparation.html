What you need to get started

  • Photo ID (passport or license)
  • Your SSN or ITIN, and those of your spouse and children if applicable
  • Income forms: W-2, 1099 or others
  • Last year’s return, if you have it
  • Bank account details for your refund
  • If you own a business: income and expense details

Depending on your case we may ask for other documents. If something is missing, we tell you what to do.

Frequently asked questions

Can I file taxes without a Social Security number?

Yes. We process your ITIN with the IRS so you can meet your tax obligations even if you do not qualify for an SSN.

I have not filed in years — can you help?

Yes. We help you get current with the IRS in an orderly way, always aiming to avoid or reduce penalties.
