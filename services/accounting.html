Frequently asked questions

How often do you do the bookkeeping?

Monthly, quarterly or annual, depending on what your business needs.

What does classifying expenses mean?

It means assigning each expense to its correct category (advertising, supplies, software, etc.). That way your deductions are well supported and your return is simpler.

Can I do the bookkeeping myself?

Yes. With My Profit & Loss you can import your bank statement and get a clean report to give your accountant. If you prefer us to do it, we can too.

Does it help with my tax return?

Yes. Organized books during the year make your return much easier, and more accurate.
